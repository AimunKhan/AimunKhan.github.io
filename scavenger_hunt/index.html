Scavenger Hunt

Rules

  • Text in group chat photo/video evidence of completing a challenge - 29 points for completing a challenge first, 20 points for second
  • For some challenges, like taking photos of strangers, if it's awkward to get a photo, we will just take your word for it
  • In case of tie, team that did more challenges wins
  • I'll spot each team $30 for their challenges if some things cost a bit of money
  • Groups must move together, no one left behind
  • Each team member must lead in at least one challenge
  • No running!
  • Do not disturb the general public
  • Meet at front of De Young by the end time - IF YOU DO NOT, YOUR TEAM IS DISQUALIFIED AND YOU LOSE AND THE OTHER TEAM WINS (jk please just try to be on time)!
  • In the first hour, you can only do challenges between Divisidero and Stanyan
  • In the final hour, you can take busses
